Aaron Inbox Assistant

A personal email assistant that runs on its owner's Mac and connects to Gmail using Google OAuth.

What it does

The assistant reads email selected by its owner, summarizes inbox messages, and highlights actions and deadlines. Labeling and archiving will be enabled only for categories explicitly approved by the owner. It currently operates in read-only mode.

Email summaries are processed with OpenAI using the owner's configured account. An optional, separately configured iMessage connection can deliver summaries to the owner.

Personal use

This application is maintained for its owner's personal use. It does not offer public registrations or access to other people's mailboxes. This website provides application information only; it cannot access Gmail.

Control over access

Google authorization can be revoked at any time in Google Account connections. Read the privacy policy for details about data processing and retention.
